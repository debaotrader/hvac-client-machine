Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what is collected when you visit this page or buy the product.

What is collected

How it is used

To deliver access, provide support, measure campaign performance and improve the page. Data is not sold.

Third parties

Checkout platform (payment and delivery), Meta (ads measurement), e-mail provider (delivery and updates) and hosting provider. Each processes data under its own policy.

Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion by writing to the contact address. Blocking cookies in the browser reduces tracking but does not prevent the purchase.

Retention

Purchase records are kept for the period required by tax and consumer law. Analytics data follows the providers' retention.

International transfers

The processors above may store data outside your country, including the United States.
